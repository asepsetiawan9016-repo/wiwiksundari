✅ Describe objects based on color, size, and shape.

A. Describing People

Describing people means telling others about someone's physical appearance and personality.

👧

Beautiful

Cantik

👦

Handsome

Tampan

💇

Long Hair

Rambut panjang

😊

Friendly

Ramah

🧑

Tall

Tinggi

🤓

Smart

Pintar

😄

Funny

Lucu

❤️

Kind

Baik hati

Example:

"She is a beautiful girl. She has long black hair. She is friendly and smart."

Dia adalah seorang gadis cantik. Dia memiliki rambut hitam panjang. Dia ramah dan pintar.
